People keep asking me how to leave a Minnesota house for a life in Florida. This page is that conversation. The first walk is The Villages, where my parents live.

The Villages, Florida

The house on the walk is theirs. It is not for sale, and I am leaving the street unnamed. If you came from the video, you are in the right place.

I live in Excelsior, on Minnewashta Park. Most of the people who ask are still here in Minnesota, often a few years out, curious what January feels like when the ordinary errands happen outside.

The Villages describes itself as a community for people 55 and older. Golf carts are a normal way to get around. The community calls them golf cars, and the paths, bridges, and tunnels are built for them. You take one to the store, to a friend, or to the square.

The town squares are where the day collects. They include Spanish Springs, Lake Sumter Landing, and Brownwood. Sawgrass Grove is a newer place to gather, and there is nightly entertainment. It moves at a different pace than a Minnesota neighborhood. That is the reason to walk it before you decide anything.

On taxes, the general version only. Florida does not tax individual income. Minnesota does. Your own tax person can say what that means for you. There is no rate on this page.

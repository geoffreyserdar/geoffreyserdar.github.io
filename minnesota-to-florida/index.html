Some families need both homes at once, and a lender may call that a bridge, and whether it fits is a question for them with your own numbers. I won't put a rate on this page, and I won't pretend there's a usual number of days, because your two closings are the timeline that matters.

What does a Florida homestead mean, and what does residency mean?

If the Florida house is the home you actually live in, you can ask the county property appraiser about a homestead exemption, which is a property-tax reduction for that house, and it isn't automatic, so you apply for it. Residency is a different question, and Florida treats you as a resident when your true, fixed, and permanent home is there, and Florida doesn't tax individual income, and Minnesota does, so your tax person should be the one who says what that means for you.

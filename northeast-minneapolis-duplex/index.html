The next ten years

The mortgage stays flat. The rent doesn't.

A fixed‑rate payment never moves, while rents usually rise. Almost every rent increase goes straight to cash flow, so the return climbs each year. At 25% down and market rents, cash‑on‑cash crosses 10% in year four.

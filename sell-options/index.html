Five real options

None of these is “the answer for everyone.” The point is to name them plainly so you are not sold a single path by a fear ad.

1 · Traditional list

Market the house on Compass MLS

Repairs, price, and timing are your call. Often the highest net when you have runway. Geoffrey runs the appointment like any other listing conversation — no pressure to pick cash.

2 · Short sale

Sell with lender approval when debt exceeds value

A process with your servicer, paperwork, and timelines that are not Geoffrey’s to invent on a webpage. He can help you understand the listing side and who else you need at the table.

3 · As-is / cash intro

One column — not the hero

When speed or condition matters, Geoffrey can introduce cash / as-is paths and still show you a traditional net for comparison. Same appointment. No “guaranteed offer in 7 days” theater.

4 · Servicer / HUD counselor

Talk to the people who can change the loan path

Call your mortgage servicer. Find a free HUD-approved counselor via hud.gov/findacounselor. Loss mitigation, forbearance, and reinstatement conversations start there — not with a bandit-sign buyer.

5 · Keep / rent bridge

Maybe you should not sell yet

Run the five-year hold-vs-sell math on /keep-or-sell/, then talk it through. Geoffrey is the agent who will tell you to keep the house when the numbers say so.

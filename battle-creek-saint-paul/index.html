Worth knowing

The short version.

District 1 is Eastview, Conway, Battle Creek, and Highwood, plus the regional park. Battle Creek has no separate city boundary.

The park is about 5 minutes, by Lower Afton and McKnight. Trails, machine-made-snow skiing, a sledding hill. Regions Hospital is about 9 minutes, at 640 Jackson Street. Downtown is about 11 minutes, and I-94 is a quick hop.

Drive about three minutes up to Suburban Avenue and half the world is on one stretch. Tea House does Sichuan cooking, with dim sum on the weekend. Los Ocampo has been serving Mexican food here for years. Sabores is a Latin market with a butcher, a bakery, and a little restaurant inside. Ha Tien sits in the old Byerly’s building, where Son and Ne Dao roast pork and duck and make bánh mì to order. They’ve been at it since 1996. If you love a little bit of everything, the globe is at your fingertips.

The creek was named in 1842 for a clash between Dakota and Ojibwe people. It runs toward Pig’s Eye Lake. Highwood, just south, is named for the Highwood station. The walls in the tour are Sherwin-Williams Shoji White, SW 7042.

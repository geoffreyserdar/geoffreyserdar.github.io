Why this one is different

Not a house with a shop. A small trades compound.

Most acreage listings are a home that happens to have a pole barn. This is the opposite: a working shop — two connected buildings, geothermal-heated, wired for heavy 3-phase equipment (through an on-site converter), on its own electric meter — that a cabinet maker built for real production. The four-bedroom home, the separate income suite, and five wooded acres come with it.

The reason that matters isn't sentiment, it's financing. A standalone commercial shop is bought with SBA or commercial money — higher rates, bigger down, a year or two of construction, and no roof over your head meanwhile. Here, the shop is attached to a home, so the whole property rides one 30-year residential loan. That's a structural advantage no bare lot can offer — and it's the entire point of the calculator below.

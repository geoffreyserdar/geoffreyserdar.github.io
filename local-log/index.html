If you are buying a home in Carver County this year, there is something worth knowing about property tax assessments that could affect your decision-making.

What Investors Should Know About Minnesota Tax-Forfeiture Properties

Recently I helped a client navigate an issue involving a tax-forfeited property purchased through a county auction in Saint Paul, Minnesota (Ramsey County).

Tax-forfeiture auctions can create opportunities for investors, but they require verification beyond what appears in county tax records.

Because the property had gone through the tax-forfeiture process, those charges should have been cleared when ownership transferred. However, shortly before closing on the resale, the assessments appeared again.

Rather than assume anything, we contacted both the county tax-forfeiture office and the city to verify the records. After reviewing the situation together, the city confirmed the charges should have been removed when the property entered forfeiture.

The issue was resolved, but it highlights an important lesson.

When purchasing tax-forfeited or auction properties in Minnesota, confirm records with both the county and the city, request written confirmation when charges are cleared, and document communication between parties.

County tax records are only part of the story.

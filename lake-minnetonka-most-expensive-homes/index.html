What “expensive” means here

  • Shoreline feet + quality — level sand vs steep; protected bay vs open fetch; dockability.
  • Bay & orientation — west/southwest exposures and privacy coves command premiums.
  • Acreage & assemblage — multi-lot compounds vs tight Cottagewood lots are different products.
  • Privacy / Quiet / off-market — ultra-asks often start quietly; years of DOM are normal.
  • Boat culture & outbuildings — boathouses, guest houses, sport courts appear in $10M+ narratives.
  • Taxes as reality check — Lake Point ~$320k/yr; other trophy asks carry six-figure taxes too.
  • List vs sold gap — historic Southways $53.5M ask vs subdivided reality; Lake Point $68→50M asks with no close yet. Asks are marketing. Comps are closings.
